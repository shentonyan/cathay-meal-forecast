## 1. Existing initiatives

### Choose My Meal (meal pre-selection)
- Since 2020, First and Business Class passengers can use the digital Choose My Meal service in the Cathay app to pre-select meals up to 10 days before boarding [Soluzioni digitali per ridurre gli sprechi alimentari](https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/it_IT/about-us/sustainability/conscious-use-of-resources/news/choose_my_meal.html "Dal 2020, i passeggeri di First e Business Class Cathay Pacific hanno la possibilità di provare il nostro nuovo servizio digitale Scegli il tuo pasto.").
- Cathay explicitly presents the service as a waste-reduction tool: passenger consumption data is used to adjust which meals are produced or loaded on later flights [Digital insights to drive down food waste](https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/about-us/sustainability/conscious-use-of-resources/news/choose_my_meal.html "insights into passenger consumption patterns and preferential meal options can be used to adjust which meals are produced or loaded onto subsequent flights which can help to reduce food waste.").
- Ordering opens 10 days before departure and closes 24 hours before, on eligible long-haul and regional flights [Choose My Meal](https://flights.cathaypacific.com/en_US/flying-with-us/inflight-dining/choose-my-meal.html "Ordering opens 10 days before the flight, and closes 24 hours before departure, for eligible long-haul and regional flights."). The official page lists only First and Business [Choose My Meal](https://flights.cathaypacific.com/en_US/flying-with-us/inflight-dining/choose-my-meal.html "Travel in First class and Business class and enjoy our Choose My Meal service.").
- In 2024 Cathay launched Cathay Signatures pre-orders of signature dishes, also on the Choose My Meal platform, currently only on selected flights from Hong Kong [Choose My Meal](https://flights.cathaypacific.com/en_US/flying-with-us/inflight-dining/choose-my-meal.html "Cathay Signatures is available on selected long-haul and regional flights departing from Hong Kong. We'll continue to expand its availability to other routes soon."); [Cathay Signatures: pre-order your favourite inflight meal](https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/inspiration/membership/cathay-signatures-pre-order-favourite-inflight-meal.html "Available for pre-order on the Choose My Meal platform, Cathay Signatures include dishes that have proven popular over the years.").

### Cathay Dining (formerly CPCS) capacity
- Older data shows CPCS catered for more than 45 airlines departing Hong Kong and produced about 83,800 meals a day (2017 figure; the current number may differ) [What happens to unused inflight food?](https://news.cathaypacific.com/what-happens-to-unused-inflight-food "Cathay Pacific Catering Services (CPCS), which handles catering for more than 45 airlines that fly from Hong Kong, produce around 83,800 meals per day.").
- CPCS has been renamed Cathay Dining [Cathay Dining increases food recycling capacity](https://www.aviationbusinessnews.com/cabin/latest-news/cathay-dining-increases-food-recycling-capacity-with-warewash-system "the Cathay Group subsidiary previously known as Cathay Pacific Catering services, has increased its food waste recycling capacity by 200%.").

### Donation partnership with Feeding Hong Kong
- Cathay was the first airline to partner with Feeding Hong Kong. Since the programme started in 2014, 2,250 tonnes of food have been collected and redistributed, benefiting 260,000 people [Cathay and Feeding Hong Kong celebrate over 10 years](https://news.cathaypacific.com/cathay-and-feeding-hong-kong-celebrate-over-10-years-of-being-a-positive-force-for-the-community-together "Since the programme's launch in 2014, 2,250 tonnes of food have been collected and redistributed to people in need, benefitting 260,000 individuals over the past decade.").
- Because of regulations, mainly sealed, non-perishable items can be donated; cooked meals usually have to be destroyed [What happens to all those uneaten inflight meals?](https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_TW/inspiration/cathay-stories/what-happens-to-all-those-uneaten-inflight-meals.html "In some countries, all unused food has to be removed and destroyed or buried deep – which makes sense for food cooked many hours previously.").

### Food Waste Segregation Warewash System (May 2025)
- Cathay Dining is the first airline caterer in Hong Kong to support recycling of food waste from inbound flights [Cathay Dining unveils an innovative Food Waste Segregation Warewash System](https://news.cathaypacific.com/cathay-dining-unveils-an-innovative-food-waste-segregation-warewash-system-with-sustainability-and-operational-enhancement-b3rrh1 "becoming the first airline caterer in Hong Kong to support the recycling of inbound food waste.").
- The system uses dedicated conveyors and compactors to separate food waste and raised recycling capacity by 200%. The waste goes to O·PARK for anaerobic digestion and power generation [Cathay Dining increases food recycling capacity](https://www.aviationbusinessnews.com/cabin/latest-news/cathay-dining-increases-food-recycling-capacity-with-warewash-system "The collected food waste is transferred to the Organic Resources Recovery Centre (O·PARK) nearby for anaerobic digestion and electricity generation."). Machine utilisation rose by more than 20% [Aircraft Interiors International](https://www.aircraftinteriorsinternational.com/news/catering-onboard/cathay-dining-launches-eco-friendly-food-waste-segregation-system.html "the newly designed system achieves over a 20% increase in machine utilisation.").
- Note: 500 tonnes a day is the processing capacity of the O·PARK facility, not Cathay's waste output [Cathay Dining unveils...](https://news.cathaypacific.com/cathay-dining-unveils-an-innovative-food-waste-segregation-warewash-system-with-sustainability-and-operational-enhancement-b3rrh1 "The O·PARK facilities can process up to 500 tonnes of food waste daily").

### ESG and waste targets
- Single-use plastics (SUP) target: from an average of 7.7 items per passenger in 2019 to 1.5 [Conscious use of resources](https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_PH/about-us/sustainability/conscious-use-of-resources.html "Reduce passenger-facing SUP items from an average of 7.7 pieces per passenger in 2019 to 1.5 pieces by 2025*."). The 2025 actual was 1.7 items, with 1.5 expected by mid-2026 [Cathay continues to advance its sustainability priorities](https://news.cathaypacific.com/cathay-continues-to-advance-its-sustainability-priorities-through-strategic-investments-and-collaborative-actions-d76ony "In 2025, the Group reduced passenger-facing SUP items in scope to an average of 1.7 pieces per passenger, with further progress towards the target of 1.5 pieces expected by mid‑2026").
- The 2023 sustainability summary mentions a target of "0.63 kg per passenger by 2030". From the context it may be a waste-intensity target, but which metric it refers to was not verified; the original report needs checking [SD one-pager 2023](https://www.cathaypacific.com/content/dam/cx/about-us/sustainability/sustainability-reports/en/cathay-pacific-sd-onepager-2023-en.pdf "to 0.63 kg per passenger by 2030").
- This search found no published total for Cathay's inflight food waste and no KPI specific to food waste.

## 2. Scale data for ROI modelling
- In 2025, Cathay Pacific and HK Express together carried more than 36 million passengers, up 27% year on year, with ASKs up more than 25% [Traffic figures December 2025](https://news.cathaypacific.com/the-cathay-group-releases-traffic-figures-for-december-2025-rlwglu "In 2025 as a whole, Cathay Pacific and HK Express carried a combined total of more than 36 million passengers, representing a 27% year-on-year increase.").
- 2025 profit attributable to shareholders was HK$10.8 billion [2025 Annual Results](https://news.cathaypacific.com/the-cathay-group-announces-2025-annual-results-cakvmh "reported an attributable profit of HK$10.8 billion in 2025, which compares with HK$9.9 billion in 2024.").
- Hong Kong hub serving more than 100 destinations [Annual Report 2025](https://www.cathaypacific.com/content/dam/cx/about-us/investor-relations/interim-annual-reports/en/2025_cx_annual_report_en.pdf "Cathay Pacific and HK Express combined offered scheduled passenger services to more than 100 destinations worldwide, including 24 in the Chinese Mainland.").
- Verified figures for daily flights, fleet size and long-haul share were not found; the fleet section of the 2025 annual report is the place to look.

## 3. Gaps and opportunities
- **Cabin coverage:** pre-selection currently covers only First and Business, and only on some routes [Choose My Meal](https://flights.cathaypacific.com/en_US/flying-with-us/inflight-dining/choose-my-meal.html "Travel in First class and Business class and enjoy our Choose My Meal service."). Premium Economy and Economy, where most passengers sit, are not covered. (Inference.)
- **Forecasting capability:** the current approach adjusts later catering based on past consumption, which is retrospective. Public material shows no AI demand model per flight and per dish. (Inference.)
- **Incentives:** no public information shows Asia Miles rewards for pre-ordering or a "no meal" option. (Inference.)
- **Data gap:** there is no public food-waste KPI, so "kg of food waste per passenger" is a natural new metric to propose.
- **Hackathon fit:** the Cathay Hackathon has run every year since 2016; 2025 was the eighth edition [8th annual Cathay Hackathon](https://news.cathaypacific.com/a-record-breaking-celebration-of-innovation-and-talent-at-the-8th-annual-cathay-hackathon-tdxm3x "Cathay proudly concluded its eighth annual Cathay Hackathon, its flagship tech event for young innovators"). Cathay describes it as a way to bring outside technology and ideas into the company [Everything you need to know about the Cathay Hackathon 2025](https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_JP/inspiration/cathay-stories/everything-need-to-know-cathay-hackathon-2024.html "Through this event we bring in external technology, ideas, and suggestions into Cathay"). The specific 2025 and 2026 themes were not verified.

## Summary
- Cathay already treats Choose My Meal as a waste-reduction tool, but it covers only First and Business on some routes. AI forecasting plus Economy pre-ordering with Asia Miles incentives fills exactly that gap.
- End-of-life handling is already mature: 2,250 tonnes donated through Feeding Hong Kong, and the 2025 segregation system tripled recycling capacity. Source reduction, deciding more precisely how many meals to load, is the relatively untouched part.
- Scale: more than 36 million Group passengers in 2025, up 27%. Older data shows Cathay Dining producing about 84,000 meals a day.
- Still needed: fleet size, daily flights, food-waste tonnage, and what the 0.63 kg per passenger target refers to.
