Choose My Meal, for every cabin

Today Choose My Meal is open to First and Business only. In this concept, every passenger can pre-order or skip their meal 10 days to 24 hours before departure and earn Asia Miles for doing it. Each choice becomes a firm demand signal for the forecasting model.

What happens behind the scenes

1
Pre-order = certain demand

Pre-ordered meals are loaded exactly. The model only has to forecast passengers who haven't chosen, so every pre-order reduces uncertainty.

2
Skip = one less meal loaded

Passengers who skip are taken out of the meal count. That cuts food and weight: .

3
The rest is forecast by AI

Gradient boosting predicts no-shows and dish shares by route, time, season and passenger mix. A newsvendor rule then sets how many of each dish to load.

Miles rewards (+50 pre-order, +150 skip) are hypotheses to A/B test; industry pre-order uptake is only about 2–5% today.

Flight forecast & loading optimiser

For each flight the model predicts the boarded passenger count and a demand distribution for each dish. A newsvendor rule then loads the quantile of total meal demand that matches the chosen service level, and splits stock across dishes to protect first choices. Below, it is compared with the legacy rule (cater for 100% of bookings × fixed network ratio + 5% buffer) over 1,000 simulated flights.
