02 How it works

01
Pre-order for every cabin
Choose My Meal opens to Economy. +50 Asia Miles to pre-order, +150 to skip the meal.
→
02
Forecast per flight, per dish
Gradient boosting predicts no-shows and dish shares. Pre-orders count as certain demand.
→
03
Optimise the load
A newsvendor rule sets each dish's quantity at a chosen service level, so nobody goes hungry.
